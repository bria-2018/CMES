01 — The business

A self-performing contractor running on scattered data.

CMES Inc. is a heavy civil and highway contractor based in Norcross, Georgia. Founded in 1996, it operates with more than 300 people and self-performs its work with its own crews — roadway construction, concrete structures and transportation electrical work.

Its operations spanned Viewpoint Vista, HCSS HeavyJob, equipment telematics, BambooHR and spreadsheets. Answering a simple business question often meant pulling information from multiple systems and stitching it together by hand.

The goal was not to replace those systems. It was to give every team a shared operational picture, while protecting the data and systems behind it.
