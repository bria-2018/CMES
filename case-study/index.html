01 — The business

A self-performing contractor running on scattered data.

CMES Inc. is a heavy civil and highway contractor based in Norcross, Georgia. Founded in 1996, it operates with more than 300 people and self-performs its work with its own crews — roadway construction, concrete structures and transportation electrical work.

Its operations spanned Viewpoint Vista, HCSS HeavyJob, equipment telematics, BambooHR and spreadsheets. Answering a simple business question often meant pulling information from multiple systems and stitching it together by hand.

The goal was not to replace those systems. It was to give every team a shared operational picture, while protecting the data and systems behind it.

Frequently asked questions

Before You Start

Answers to common questions about connecting ERP, field systems, and AI in heavy civil operations.

Can AI be used with financial and HR data?

Yes. At CMES the assistants are read-only, respect role-based permissions so people only see approved data, and log every question.

Does this replace Viewpoint Vista or HCSS HeavyJob?

No. Viewpoint Vista remains the system of record. The portal connects Vista, HeavyJob, telematics and BambooHR, then adds reporting and AI on top.

How is the 3,000+ hours-saved figure calculated?

Hours per run × runs per year × people involved, added up across every automated task — payroll checks, finance and fleet reports, HR lookups and document searches. The total is as reported by the CMES team.

Can you add AI to our existing systems?

Usually, yes. We connect to the systems you already use and ground assistants in your approved documents and records using retrieval-augmented generation.

How long does a project like this take?

The CMES platform was delivered over nine months in phases, module by module, with ongoing work on new features and integrations.

What does a custom operations platform cost?

It depends on the number of systems, reports and users involved. An operations audit maps your highest-impact workflows first so you can scope a phased budget.

Can you support a team without in-house developers?

Yes. After launch we monitor operations and keep improving reports, security and AI workflows as the business grows.

Who owns the code and the data?

Your data stays in your systems and under your control. Code ownership terms are agreed in the engagement contract.
