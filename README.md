## 🛠️ Key Highlights

1. **Executive Storytelling & Visual Hierarchy**:
   - High-contrast sections alternating between dark luxury backgrounds (`#0c0d0f`), crisp white reading containers, and warm neutral cards.
   - 1320px maximum container width matching Commerce Pundit corporate design standards.

2. **Audience-Driven Navigation**:
   - Dedicated journeys for **Public Sector & DOT Clients**, **Prospective Employees & Field Crews**, and **Subcontractors & Partners**.
   - Equal-height dual showcases highlighting project case studies alongside career & safety workflows.

3. **Enterprise Technology Stack**:
   - Real brand SVG logos across Web & Digital Experience (WordPress, Custom Theme, SVG Icons), Field & Operations (Trimble, HCSS, BambooHR, Telematics), Data & Cloud Infrastructure (AWS, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, .NET, Angular), and Intelligence & AI (Claude, OpenAI).

4. **WordPress Theme Ready**:
   - Complete `single-pf-cmes-case-study.php` template matching Commerce Pundit's child theme conventions (`single-pf-*.php`).
